# Stage 0 — Problem Validation

## Objective

Validate that the problem is real, painful, frequent, and commercially meaningful before significant development.

## Current hypothesis

Dental records are fragmented between clinics and countries.

Patients may:
- lose documents
- carry PDFs/images manually
- forget treatment details
- struggle to transfer history
- have difficulty proving implant/treatment information
- receive treatment in one country and follow-up elsewhere

Clinics may:
- receive incomplete records
- spend time reading unstructured documents
- manually enter historical information
- lack implant details
- struggle with cross-clinic/cross-country continuity

## Why Albania matters

Albania is a useful initial market hypothesis because dental tourism creates cross-border treatment.

Example:

Italy → Albania → treatment → Italy

The patient's information needs to travel with them.

## Research tasks

### Patient research
Investigate:
- complaints about dental records
- problems changing dentists
- cross-border treatment issues
- implant/warranty problems
- document organization
- patient understanding of dental records

### Dentist/clinic research
Investigate:
- time spent collecting history
- manual data entry
- document processing
- missing implant information
- foreign-patient workflows
- follow-up problems after dental tourism

### Market research
Investigate:
- dental PMS/CRM products
- patient health-record products
- dental passport products
- implant manufacturer systems
- interoperability solutions
- Albania dental-tourism market
- major source countries

## Evidence standard

Do not rely on one Reddit post or one interview.

For every important problem, record:
- Source
- Problem
- Who experiences it
- Frequency indication
- Severity indication
- Existing workaround
- Existing software
- Why current solution is insufficient

## Important questions

1. Who has the strongest pain?
2. What exact workflow causes the pain?
3. How often does it happen?
4. How much time/money does it cost?
5. Who pays to solve it?
6. Would clinics adopt an external platform?
7. Would patients use it independently?
8. Does dental tourism create a strong initial wedge?
9. What competitors already solve this?
10. What is the smallest valuable MVP?

## Decision gate

Do not proceed with broad product development unless there is evidence that:
- the problem is real
- current solutions are insufficient
- there is a reachable initial market
- the proposed MVP can solve a meaningful part of the problem

## Deliverables

- Research notes
- Evidence table
- Competitor map
- Albania market context
- Ranked pain points
- Initial customer hypotheses
- MVP hypothesis
- Risk list

## Exit criteria

We can explain in one paragraph:

> Who has the problem, what the problem is, why it matters, why existing approaches are insufficient, and why our proposed MVP is worth testing.

## Decisions

### D0-001 — Albania as starting wedge
**Status:** Working hypothesis

Albania is useful as an initial market because of dental tourism, but the architecture should not be Albania-specific.

### D0-002 — Focus on real problems
**Status:** Confirmed

Research should prioritize pain and operational problems rather than starting from a technology or user-growth idea.
