# Stage 9 — International Expansion

## Objective

Expand from the initial market into international dental care and cross-border treatment.

## Initial expansion hypothesis

Albania ↔ Italy is a natural corridor to investigate because of cross-border dental care and geographic proximity.

Later possibilities:
- Other EU countries
- Additional dental-tourism corridors
- International clinic groups

## Localization areas

- Language
- Currency
- Date formats
- Tooth notation
- Clinical terminology
- Privacy requirements
- Document formats
- Consent requirements

## Regulatory areas

Before international deployment, investigate:
- Health-data privacy
- Cross-border data transfers
- Patient rights
- Retention/deletion
- Consent
- AI processing
- Applicable medical software rules

Do not assume that a technical design is automatically legally compliant.

## Business model options

Keep pricing open until pilot evidence exists.

Potential models:
- Clinic subscription
- Per-patient pricing
- AI/document usage
- Premium patient features
- Enterprise clinic groups
- Integrations

## International product principle

The passport should be portable across countries without becoming tied to one country's clinic workflow.

## Exit criteria

There is evidence of repeatable demand outside the initial market and a viable operational, legal, and commercial expansion plan.
