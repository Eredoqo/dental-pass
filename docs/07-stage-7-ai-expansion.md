# Stage 7 — AI Expansion

## Objective

Expand AI only after the core document workflow is proven.

## Candidate features

### Document intelligence
- Better extraction
- Document classification
- Duplicate detection
- Missing-information detection

### Dental timeline
AI creates a readable history from verified records.

### Patient explanation
Convert technical records into understandable language.

### Clinic assistant
Answer questions about information already present in the patient's records.

## Example

```text
Verified records
      ↓
AI
      ↓
"Patient had implant placement on tooth 14
in March 2026. Manufacturer: ..."
      ↓
Links to source records
```

## Safety boundary

Do not treat AI-generated clinical interpretation as verified medical fact.

Avoid autonomous:
- diagnosis
- treatment decisions
- prescriptions
- clinical recommendations

unless a separate clinical/regulatory strategy is established.

## AI metrics

Track:
- Accuracy
- Correction rate
- Confidence
- Processing time
- Cost/document
- Human review time
- Failure rate

## Exit criteria

AI demonstrably saves time or improves understanding without unacceptable clinical or operational risk.
